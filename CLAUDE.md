# Phone Booth Kit

Read `GUIDE.md` in this folder before doing anything, and follow it.

The person you are helping is at a projection mapping workshop. They will give you a photo of a real surface and one sentence about what they want the light to do. Your job is to write a new `index.html` in this folder that loads `sense.js` and `map.js`, as `GUIDE.md` describes.

- Never edit `sense.js` or `map.js`.
- Before overwriting the example `index.html`, ask whether they want to keep it. If they do, write to a new file name instead.
- Ask the person for the FOR line of the SENSE / DO / FOR comment. Do not invent it.
- Reply in the language the person writes in.
